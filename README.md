### Hi, I'm Yupeng Tang

I am a researcher and engineer working across LLMs, multimodal AI, ML systems, and robotics. I recently graduated from Georgia Tech with dual M.S. degrees in Computer Science (Machine Learning) and Computational Science & Engineering (Applied Mathematics), and I currently work as a Robotics Engineer at Pudu Robotics.

My research focuses on foundation model reliability and efficient AI systems. I study how large language models and vision-language models use evidence, revise decisions, represent uncertainty, and behave as inference-time compute scales. Recent work spans multimodal RAG, self-correction, RLVR, uncertainty estimation, efficient VLM inference, and visuomotor learning. I am particularly interested in settings where stronger models, additional context, or more compute do not automatically translate into more reliable behavior.

On the systems side, I build GPU-optimized inference pipelines, large-scale evaluation infrastructure, and production serving systems for generative models. Previously at GMI Cloud, I optimized Flux-Schnell (12B DiT) inference on H100 GPUs to ~30 images/min at 1–2s latency and worked on multi-GPU inference and serving with TensorRT and NCCL.

I also contribute to open-source ML infrastructure, with bug fixes, tests, and systems improvements merged upstream into PyTorch/TorchRL, Hugging Face Transformers and Accelerate, Liger-Kernel, and Axolotl.

I grew up in China and did my undergraduate degree in Artificial Intelligence at Shandong University. Those four years gave me a strong grounding in mathematics and control theory, and more importantly, taught me how to think across disciplinary boundaries. I have carried that habit with me ever since.
